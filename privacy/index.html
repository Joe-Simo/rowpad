Rowpad

Privacy Policy

Rowpad does not collect any data. Your projects stay on your device.

Effective October 29, 2026.

What Rowpad stores

Your projects, row counts, counters, and notes are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage and in an App Group shared with Rowpad's widgets and Lock Screen counter. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else.

What Rowpad does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising.
  • No third-party SDKs.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access.

Purchases

Rowpad Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, email, or payment details. The app checks your purchase with Apple on your device so it can unlock Pro features. Apple's handling of purchase information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Siri, Shortcuts, widgets, and Live Activities

These features run on your device using Apple's system frameworks. Rowpad does not send their data anywhere.

Backups and deleting your data

If you use iCloud Backup or a computer backup, your device backup may include Rowpad's data, under Apple's terms. To delete everything Rowpad stores, delete the app from your iPhone.

Rowpad for Android

Rowpad for Android works the same way as Rowpad for iPhone and iPad. It has no account and no server, and it contains no ads, analytics, or tracking. Your projects, rows, counters, notes, daily progress, and settings are stored only on your device.

Purchases. Rowpad Pro is bought through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment, and Rowpad never sees your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether your Google account owns Rowpad Pro. Google Play Billing, a component provided by Google, communicates with Google Play to complete and confirm purchases and may send Google diagnostic information about the billing library. That information goes to Google under Google's privacy policy, not to us.

Backup. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include your Rowpad projects in that backup so they come back on a new phone. You control this in Android settings. We cannot read your backup.

Widget, Quick Settings tile, and lock screen counter. These read and change your data on your device only. The lock screen counter is a notification you turn on in the app; Android asks for notification permission the first time.

Deleting your data. Uninstalling Rowpad deletes its data from your device.

Rowpad for Windows

Rowpad for Windows stores your projects (names, notes, row counts, repeats, targets, extra counters), your daily row history, the pattern images you add, and your settings only on your PC, in the app's local browser storage (IndexedDB). You can delete a project or a pattern image in the app, or use Settings, Delete all data, to remove everything. When you uninstall, Windows may ask whether to also clear the app's data; choose that to remove everything.

Rowpad for Windows has no account and no sign-in, and no telemetry, analytics, ads, or tracking. It does not use your location, contacts, microphone, or camera.

Pattern images are only the picture files you choose, drag in, or paste. They stay on your PC and are never uploaded.

Rowpad for Windows makes no network requests except to Microsoft Store billing, to check or complete a Rowpad Pro purchase, and to download its own app files and updates from joe-simo.github.io (hosted by GitHub Pages). Your projects and pattern images are never included in any request. Microsoft handles purchases under the Microsoft Privacy Statement; Rowpad never sees your payment details.

The Windows widget (Pro) is filled in on your PC from the same local data, and its "Add a row" button changes only that local data. Nothing is sent to a server.

Backup (Pro) saves one file, including your pattern images, where you choose. Restore reads only the file you pick. Rowpad does not upload either.

"Keep screen on" only asks Windows to keep the display awake while Rowpad is open on screen.

Children

Rowpad does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
