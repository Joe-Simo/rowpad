Rowpad

Privacy Policy

Rowpad does not collect any data. Your projects stay on your iPhone.

Effective October 25, 2026.

What Rowpad stores

Your projects, row counts, counters, and notes are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage and in an App Group shared with Rowpad's widgets and Lock Screen counter. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else.

What Rowpad does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising.
  • No third-party SDKs.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access.

Purchases

Rowpad Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, email, or payment details. The app checks your purchase with Apple on your device so it can unlock Pro features. Apple's handling of purchase information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Siri, Shortcuts, widgets, and Live Activities

These features run on your device using Apple's system frameworks. Rowpad does not send their data anywhere.

Backups and deleting your data

If you use iCloud Backup or a computer backup, your device backup may include Rowpad's data, under Apple's terms. To delete everything Rowpad stores, delete the app from your iPhone.

Children

Rowpad does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
