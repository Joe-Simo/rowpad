Counting rows

Open a project and tap anywhere on the screen to add a row. Use the minus button to go back one. Set a pattern repeat to see where you are in it, and a target to see how many rows are left.

Lock Screen counter and widgets

With Rowpad Pro, tap the Lock Screen button in a project to keep a counter on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, so you can add rows without unlocking. To add a Home Screen or Lock Screen widget, touch and hold your Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Rowpad.

Siri and the Action Button

With Rowpad Pro, say "Add a row in Rowpad". You can also assign the Add Row shortcut to the Action Button in Settings, Action Button, Shortcut.

Purchases

Rowpad Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. If Pro isn't unlocked after buying, open Settings in Rowpad and tap Restore Purchase while signed in with the same Apple Account. For refund requests, visit Apple's purchase support.

Your data

Rowpad keeps your projects only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes its data. See the Privacy Policy.
